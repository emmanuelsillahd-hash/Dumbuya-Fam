## 3. Stage 3.3.1 Implementation Plan Summary

1. **Authoritative Data Model & Seeding (`src/server/backupAndMigration.ts`)**:
   - Extend `AuthoritativeWorkspace` with `motto`, `mission`, `vision`, `values`, `logoUrl`, `photoUrl`, `announcements`, `reminders`, and `approvedAiSuggestions`.
   - Add `AuthoritativeFamilyMember`, `AuthoritativeInvitation`, `AuthoritativeFamilyRelationship`, and `AuthoritativeMediaAsset` collections to `SharedStore` and seed rich initial records for `fam-1` while maintaining pre-3.2 financial reconciliation.
2. **Zero-Trust RBAC, One-Family Rule & Privacy Filtering (`src/server/authAndRbac.ts`, `server.ts`, `firestore.rules`)**:
   - Enforce the one-active-family membership rule on family creation (`POST /api/v1/workspaces`), invitation response (`POST /api/v1/invitations/:invitationId/respond`), and membership approval (`POST /api/v1/memberships/:membershipId/approve`).
   - Block client self-promotion to `OWNER`/`ADMIN` across all member/membership endpoints.
   - Redact `personalInfo` on member profiles for non-owner/non-self callers and redact financial summaries for `CHILD`/`GUEST` roles.
   - Add authenticated family media upload and retrieval (`POST /api/v1/media/:familyId/upload`, `GET /api/v1/media/:familyId/:mediaId`) with family isolation and personal vs. shared visibility enforcement.
   - Synchronize `FAMILY_WORKSPACE`, `FAMILY_MEMBER`, `FAMILY_RELATIONSHIP`, `INVITATION`, and `MEDIA_ASSET` through `/api/v1/sync/push` and `/api/v1/sync/pull`.
3. **Android Implementation (`DashboardScreen.kt`, `FamilyTreeScreen.kt`, `FamilyOSViewModel.kt`, `MainActivity.kt`)**:
   - Connect `DashboardScreen.kt` (Family Home) and `FamilyTreeScreen.kt` (People & Relationships) 100% to live Room & ViewModel state, removing static demonstration lists and adding interactive profile editing, relationship selection, invitation/role management, and remote sync reconciliation.
4. **Web Implementation (`src/App.tsx`)**:
   - Implement live, authoritative **Family Home** and **People & Relationships** views connected to `/api/v1/workspace/:familyId` and all Stage 3.3.1 endpoints, with role/persona switching to verify privacy and permissions directly in the UI.
5. **Automated Verification (`scripts/verify-stage-3-3-1.ts`)**:
   - Verify all 12 mandatory test areas (family creation, member invitation, invitation acceptance, membership approval, role enforcement, member removal/revocation, profile updates, family isolation, personal record privacy, cross-platform consistency, offline synchronization, and image storage access) plus all 22 existing Stage 3.2 gates.
